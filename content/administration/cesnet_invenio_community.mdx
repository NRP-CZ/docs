# CESNET Invenio Community

The CESNET Invenio Community brings together people who develop and run repositories built on CESNET Invenio — a core repository system of the Czech National Repository Platform (NRP). It is a space for developers, operators, data curators and other repository roles from institutions across Czech academia.

The community works both ways: the CESNET Invenio development team passes on codebase updates and implementation recommendations, and members raise questions, share practical experience, propose new topics — and bring feedback that feeds into the system's roadmap.


## Meetings

* **CESNET Invenio Community meetings** take place on the **first Wednesday** of every month at **11:00**.

* Upcoming meetings are listed in the shared **calendar** available at the [EOSC RepoBuilders community page](https://www.eosc.cz/en/communities/repobuilders#kalendar_repobuilders).

*The calendar contains also the quarterly meetings of the broader [RepoBuilders community](https://docs.nrp.eosc.cz/en/docs/repo_admins/community/repobuilders) that cover topics spanning repository systems.*
