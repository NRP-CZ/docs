# CESNET Invenio Community

The CESNET Invenio Community is the system-specific level of the RepoBuilders community. Where the general RepoBuilders level covers topics across repository systems — methodologies, standards and practical procedures — this level focuses on CESNET Invenio.


The point is sharing experience: a space for operators, developers and other roles from various institutions to compare what works, help each other over the same obstacles, and turn recurring open questions into shared understanding rather than isolated answers.

## Meetings

* **CESNET Invenio Community meetings** take place on the first Wednesday of every month at 11:00. Topics cover CESNET Invenio system-specific updates, the roadmap, Q&A, and proposals for new topics of interest.
* The quarterly **RepoBuilders meetings** are described on the [RepoBuilders Community page](https://docs.nrp.eosc.cz/en/docs/repo_admins/community/repobuilders).

Upcoming dates for both are listed in the shared calendar of events on [EOSC repo builders community page](https://www.eosc.cz/komunity/repobuilders).
